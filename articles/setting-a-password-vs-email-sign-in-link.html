Setting a Password vs. Using an Email Sign-In Link

Two ways to sign in to your Fomo Leads dashboard.

You don't create a password at signup. Signup only asks for your full name, email and phone. After that you can sign in either way.

Option 1: Email sign-in link

  1. Go to https://members.fomoleads.io/login.
  2. Enter your email and click Email me a sign-in link.
  3. Open the email and click the link.

Option 2: Set a password

  1. Go to https://members.fomoleads.io/settings and find Password: "Set a password to sign in without an email link."
  2. Enter your New password and Confirm password.
  3. Click Set password.

Can't find the sign-in email? Check your spam or promotions folder, or email support@fomoleads.io.
