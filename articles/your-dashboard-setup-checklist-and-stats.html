Your Dashboard: Setup Checklist and Stats

What every card on your dashboard means.

Your dashboard lives at https://members.fomoleads.io/dashboard.

Finish account setup

Until setup is complete, you'll see a checklist:

  • Choose states: "Required before any new leads can route to you."
  • Add funds: "Your wallet balance determines how many leads you can receive."

Your stats

Card What it shows
Wallet balance Funds available for leads
Leads received Leads delivered to you
Active states States you're covering
Est. leads remaining Roughly how many leads your balance covers
Leads worked, Contact rate, Booking rate How you're working your leads

Closer stats

Your dashboard also tracks AP Closed, Commission, Average AP, Profit, CAC, ROI, AP ROAS and Lead to Close. Use the I closed a deal button to record a sale (see Tracking closes and ROI).

"You're out of leads"

If your wallet is empty, you'll see: "You're out of leads. Your wallet is empty, so no new leads are being sent to you right now." Click Add funds to resume delivery.
