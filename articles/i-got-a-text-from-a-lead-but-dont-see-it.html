I Got a Text From a Lead, But I Don't See the Lead in My Dashboard

What to do if a lead texts you before showing up in Fomo Leads.

Leads can text the number on your thank-you page right after they submit, so a text can arrive before you see the lead.

  1. Refresh https://members.fomoleads.io/leads and check that the date filter is set to All time.
  1. If you use a CRM webhook, check your CRM. Leads are sent with the event lead.assigned.
  2. If it's still missing, email support@fomoleads.io with the phone number and the time of the text.
