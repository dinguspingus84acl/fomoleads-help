Turning On Push Lead Alerts

Get a browser notification when a lead is assigned to you.

Fomo Leads sends a browser push notification whenever a lead is assigned to you. Look for the Lead alerts section at https://members.fomoleads.io/settings.

  1. Open https://members.fomoleads.io/settings.
  2. When your browser asks to show notifications, click Allow.

Notifications blocked?

Turn them on in your device's Settings › Notifications › Fomo Leads, then reload the page.

Note: Lead alerts are push only. There are no email or SMS lead notifications.
