I'm Getting Leads in States I'm Not Licensed In

Check your coverage states, and what to do about an out-of-state lead.

  1. Go to Coverage states in https://members.fomoleads.io/settings. Make sure only your licensed states are selected, then click Save states.
  1. If a lead still arrives from outside your states, email support@fomoleads.io with the lead's details. Refunds other than quality refunds are reviewed case by case.
