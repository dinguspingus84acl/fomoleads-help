Setting Your Daily Leads and the Busy-Day +10

Control how many leads you get each day.

  1. Go to https://members.fomoleads.io/settings.
  2. Move the Daily leads slider. The default is 15.
  3. Click Save daily leads.

You'll see your cap, for example: "Your cap today: 15, +10 on a busy day."

What's the busy-day +10?

"On a busy day, when nobody else covering your states has room, we may send you up to 10 more verified leads, charged at your normal price."

Things that affect your daily volume

  • Your wallet balance. Delivery stops at $0.
  • Your plan's ad-spend cap.
  • Your coverage states and how the campaigns perform.
