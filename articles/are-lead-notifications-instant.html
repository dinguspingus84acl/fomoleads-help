Are Lead Notifications Instant?

Push alerts go out when a lead is assigned to you.

Yes. Fomo Leads sends a browser push notification when a lead is assigned to you. If alerts don't arrive, enable notifications in your device's Settings › Notifications › Fomo Leads and reload. There are no email or SMS alerts.
