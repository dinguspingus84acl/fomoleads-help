Plans and Ad-Spend Caps

Compare the trial and the Launch, Grow and Scale plans.

"Choose a plan to unlock funding. Leads are paid from your wallet."

Plan Price Ad spend per period Best for
Trial $247 once Up to $1,000 Trying Fomo, no subscription
Launch $597 / 30 days Up to $3,000 One agent getting started
Grow (Recommended) $997 / 30 days Up to $6,000 Steady daily flow for a full-time producer
Scale $1,497 / 30 days Up to $10,000 Teams and top producers

How caps work

  • Ad-spend caps reset every billing period.
  • Lead volume depends on your states and how the campaigns perform.
  • Leads are paid from your wallet at your locked rate.
  • Unused wallet funds stay in your wallet.

Manage your plan at https://members.fomoleads.io/wallet?billing=manage (Manage or cancel billing).
