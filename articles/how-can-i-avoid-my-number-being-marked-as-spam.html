How Can I Avoid My Phone Number Being Marked as Spam?

Spam labels come from your phone or dialer provider. Here's what helps.

Phone carriers and dialer providers apply spam labels, not Fomo Leads. These help:

  • Use a registered business number in your dialer or CRM, and rotate numbers if one gets flagged.
  • Call from the number leads see on your thank-you page. Set it under Number your leads text in https://members.fomoleads.io/settings.
  • Reach out quickly. Push lead alerts tell you when a lead is assigned to you.
