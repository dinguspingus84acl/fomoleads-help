How Long Does It Take to Get Leads?

What needs to be in place before leads start.

Leads can start once both setup steps on your dashboard are done:

  • Choose states: "Required before any new leads can route to you."
  • Add funds: "Your wallet balance determines how many leads you can receive."

Once setup is finished, your first lead typically arrives in under 24 hours. After that, lead volume depends on your states and how the campaigns perform.
