Adding Funds to Your Wallet and Locked Lead Rates

How your prepaid wallet pays for leads, and how your rate is locked.

Every lead is paid for from your prepaid wallet.

Adding funds

  1. Go to https://members.fomoleads.io/wallet. Adding funds unlocks once you've chosen a trial or plan.
  2. Enter an amount between $500 and $10,000, or click Max.
  3. The page shows how many leads that buys at your current rate. For example, at a rate of $21.63, $1,500 is about 69 Mortgage Protection leads.
  4. Complete checkout. Payments are processed by Whop.

Locked lead rates

Lead category Price per lead
Mortgage Protection Shown on your Wallet page ($21.63 as of October 2026), "Locked for the funds you add today."
Final Expense, General Term, IUL Coming soon

The rate can change over time, so always check Your Mortgage Protection rate on the Wallet page. Your rate locks each time you add funds. Each load is a prepaid tranche, and tranches are used oldest first. So leads always come out of your earliest funds, at the rate locked for those funds.

When your wallet is empty

Delivery stops automatically. Your dashboard shows: "You're out of leads. Your wallet is empty, so no new leads are being sent to you right now." Click Add funds to resume delivery.

Tip: Keep an eye on Est. leads remaining on your dashboard so you can top up before you run out.
