Exporting Leads to CSV

Download your leads as a spreadsheet.

  1. Go to https://members.fomoleads.io/leads.
  1. Set the date filter (for example All time).
  2. Click Export CSV.
  1. Open the file in Excel, Google Sheets or your CRM's import tool.
