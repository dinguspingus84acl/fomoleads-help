Choosing Your States

Tell us where you're licensed so you only get leads there.

Choosing states is required before any new leads can route to you.

  1. Go to https://members.fomoleads.io/settings and find Coverage states.
  2. Select your licensed states. You can pick from all 50 states plus DC. All and Clear help you select quickly.
  3. Click Save states.

There's nothing to relaunch, because we run the ads for you.

See how each state is doing: https://members.fomoleads.io/states shows a per-state performance table (see Viewing state performance).
