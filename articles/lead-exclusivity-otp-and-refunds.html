Lead Exclusivity, OTP Verification and Refunds

What makes a Fomo Leads lead, and how refunds work.

Exclusive

"Every lead is yours. Real-time, fresh, exclusive leads — yours for life, never resold." The Client Lead Agreement guarantees 30-day lead exclusivity.

OTP-verified

Every lead confirmed their phone number with a one-time code before submitting.

Refunds

  • No quality refunds or replacements are given on OTP-verified leads.
  • Other refund requests are reviewed case by case. Email support@fomoleads.io with the lead's name and the date it arrived.
  • Unused wallet funds stay in your wallet.
