Welcome to Fomo Leads: How It Works

A quick overview of how Fomo Leads gets exclusive leads to you.

Welcome to Fomo Leads! We're a done-for-you lead service for insurance agents. You don't build campaigns, manage an ad account or set ad budgets. We do that part.

How it works

  1. Pick a plan and add funds.
  1. We run Meta ads in your states.
  2. Leads land in your member area and CRM, billed at your locked rate.

What leads are available?

Mortgage Protection leads are live. Your per-lead rate is shown on the Wallet page ($21.63 as of October 2026) and is locked each time you add funds. Final Expense, General Term and IUL are coming soon, and you can join the waitlist for each one. See Lead categories and the waitlist.

Every lead is yours

"Real-time, fresh, exclusive leads — yours for life, never resold." Every lead is OTP-verified, which means they confirmed their phone number with a one-time code.
