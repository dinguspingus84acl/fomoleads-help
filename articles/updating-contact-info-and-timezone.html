Updating Your Contact Info and Timezone

Change your name, phone and timezone.

Contact info

  1. Go to https://members.fomoleads.io/settings and find Contact info.
  2. Update your Full name or Phone.
  3. Click Save contact.

To change your email, contact support at support@fomoleads.io.

Timezone

Pick ET, CT, MT, AZ, PT, AKT or HT, then click Save timezone.
