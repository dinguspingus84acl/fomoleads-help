How the $247 Trial Works

Try Fomo Leads once, with no subscription.

Try Fomo for $247. "Pay $247 once, then load up to $1,000 in ad spend and get leads in your states. No subscription."

How to start

  1. Go to https://members.fomoleads.io/wallet.
  2. Click Start my trial for $247. Payments are processed by Whop.
  1. Add funds to your wallet. Wallet funds are separate from the $247.
  2. Choose your states at https://members.fomoleads.io/settings.

Good to know

  • No recurring charge. The trial doesn't renew.
  • The $247 doesn't become wallet funds. Your leads are paid from what you load into your wallet, at the Mortgage Protection rate shown on your Wallet page ($21.63 per lead as of October 2026), locked when you add funds.
  • The trial covers up to $1,000 in ad spend. For more, pick a plan (see Plans and ad-spend caps).
