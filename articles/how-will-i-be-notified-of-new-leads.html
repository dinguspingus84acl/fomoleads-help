How Will I Be Notified of New Leads?

Where new leads show up and how you're alerted.

  • Browser push alerts when a lead is assigned to you (see Turning on push lead alerts).
  • Your member area: every lead appears at https://members.fomoleads.io/leads.
  • Your CRM: connect a webhook at https://members.fomoleads.io/integrations and leads are sent there automatically.

Fomo Leads does not send email or SMS lead notifications.
