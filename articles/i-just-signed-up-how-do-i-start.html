I Just Signed Up. How Do I Start?

Your first-day checklist, from agreement to first lead.

Here's everything to do on day one, in order.

  1. Accept the Client Lead Agreement on the welcome screen. Type your full name, tick both boxes and click Agree and continue. This step doesn't charge your payment method.
  2. Choose a trial or plan at https://members.fomoleads.io/wallet. Click Start my trial for $247, or pick Launch, Grow or Scale.
  1. Add funds at https://members.fomoleads.io/wallet. Funding unlocks once you've picked a trial or plan.
  1. Choose your states at https://members.fomoleads.io/settings under Coverage states, then click Save states. This is required before any new leads can come to you.
  1. Set your daily leads at https://members.fomoleads.io/settings, then click Save daily leads.
  1. Set up your thank-you page profile at https://members.fomoleads.io/settings. Add your photo, the number your leads text, and your contact card.
  1. Allow lead alerts in your browser so you're notified when a lead is assigned to you.
  1. (Optional) Connect your CRM at https://members.fomoleads.io/integrations, or add reps at https://members.fomoleads.io/team.

Your dashboard at https://members.fomoleads.io/dashboard shows a Finish account setup checklist so you can see what's left.
