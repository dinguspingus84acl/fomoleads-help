Lead Categories and the Waitlist

Why you only see Mortgage Protection, and how to join the waitlist.

Mortgage Protection is live. Your current per-lead rate is shown on the Wallet page ($21.63 as of October 2026) and is locked when you add funds.

These are coming soon:

  • Final Expense
  • General Term
  • IUL

To get on the list, go to https://members.fomoleads.io/wallet and click Join the waitlist next to the category you want.
