Adding Reps and Round-Robin Lead Distribution

Share leads across your team automatically.

"Reps receive leads via fair-queue rotation and can disposition them."

  1. Go to https://members.fomoleads.io/team.
  2. Click Add rep.

"Add a rep and they'll receive leads via round-robin rotation." Each new lead goes to the next rep in line, and reps can update the outcome of their leads.
