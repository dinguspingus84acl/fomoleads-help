Does the Thank-You Page Show the Number I'll Be Calling From?

It shows the number you set in Settings.

The thank-you page shows the Number your leads text that you saved at https://members.fomoleads.io/settings, along with your contact card. Use the number you'll call from so leads recognize you. See Setting up your thank-you page profile and text number.
