Accepting the Client Lead Agreement

What the agreement covers, and why accepting it doesn't charge you.

After you sign up, you'll be asked to accept the FOMO Leads Client Lead Agreement. This is the agreement between you and FOMO Leads LLC.

How to accept it

  1. Type your full name.
  2. Tick "I have read and agree...".
  3. Tick "I am authorized to accept this agreement for this account and business."
  4. Click Agree and continue.

"This step does not charge your payment method. You'll choose your package and funding separately." You'll then land on your wallet page.

Key terms in plain English

  • Your membership renews every 30 days.
  • Membership fees don't become wallet funds. Leads are paid from your wallet separately.
  • Wallet loads are prepaid tranches, used oldest first.
  • Leads are exclusive to you for 30 days under the agreement.
  • There are no quality refunds or replacements on OTP-verified leads. Other refund requests are reviewed case by case through support@fomoleads.io.
  • Payments are processed by Whop.
  • You can cancel future renewals with Manage or cancel billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your paid period.

Finding your copy later

At https://members.fomoleads.io/settings, the Lead agreement section shows your version and who accepted it on which date. It also has Download accepted copy and Read current agreement.
