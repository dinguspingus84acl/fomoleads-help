How Do I Cancel or Manage My Billing?

Cancel future renewals or update billing.

  1. Go to https://members.fomoleads.io/wallet?billing=manage. You can also click Manage or cancel billing in the Lead agreement section of https://members.fomoleads.io/settings.
  1. Manage your plan or cancel future renewals there. Payments are processed by Whop.

Good to know

  • Plans renew every 30 days.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals. It takes effect at the end of your paid period.
  • Unused wallet funds stay in your wallet.
  • Membership fees don't become wallet funds.

Questions? Email support@fomoleads.io.
