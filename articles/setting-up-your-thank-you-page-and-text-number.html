Setting Up Your Thank-You Page Profile and Text Number

Customize what leads see and the text they send you.

After a lead submits, they see your thank-you page. Set it up in the Thank-you page profile section at https://members.fomoleads.io/settings.

  1. Upload photo: add a headshot.
  2. License number: enter it and click Save license number.
  1. Number your leads text: enter your number, or click Use my account number. Click Send a test text to check it, then Save number.
  1. Text message your leads send you: edit the prefilled message (up to 300 characters). You can use these tokens: {lead_first_name}, {rep_first_name} and {state}.
  1. Contact card: fill in Display name, Title, Company, Email, Website and Phone, and check the live preview.

Reset steps restores the defaults. Changes apply to new leads from then on.
