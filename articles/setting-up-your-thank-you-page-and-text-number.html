Set Up Your Thank-You Page

Add your photo, license number, text number, message and contact card so new leads can reach you right away.

Your thank-you page is what a new lead sees the moment they submit their form: your photo, your name and your license number, plus a text they can send you in one tap and your contact card. Set it up in Settings → Your thank-you page profile (https://members.fomoleads.io/settings).

💾 Each part has its own save button. Nothing goes live until you click it.

🖼️ 1. Photo and license number

Click Upload photo and pick a clear, well-lit headshot (it's cropped to a circle). Then type your License number and click Save license number.

  • Your license shows as License No. … under your name and on your contact card. Leave it blank to hide it.
  • No photo? Leads see your initials instead.

📱 2. Number your leads text

This is the number behind the Text and Save contact buttons on your thank-you page. Your account cell stays as it is.

  • Leave Use my account number checked to use the phone on your account.
  • Or uncheck it and type a different US mobile number that can receive texts in Number for lead texts. Then click Save number.
  • Send a test text opens Messages on your phone, to your saved number, with your saved message. Use it from your phone after you save.

💬 3. The text message your leads send you

This message is prefilled in the lead's Messages app when they tap Text [your first name] now. They can edit it before sending.

Placeholder Becomes Example
{lead_first_name} The lead's first name Sarah
{rep_first_name} Your first name (from your display name) Jordan
{state} The lead's state Texas
  • Click where you want it in the message, then click the placeholder button to insert it.
  • Up to 300 characters. The counter shows how many you've used, and the page shows the sent text for a sample lead.
  • The default message is: Hi {rep_first_name}, it's {lead_first_name}. I just got pre-qualified for mortgage protection and I'd like to see my options.
  • After you edit it, a Reset to default link appears under the box if you want the original text back.
  • Click Save message.

🪪 4. Your contact card

This is what lands in the lead's contacts when they tap Save [your name]'s contact. Your display name is also the name on your thank-you page.

  • Fill in Display name and Title. Company, Email and Website are optional.
  • Phone comes from Number your leads text.
  • Your license number is added to the card's notes. With no photo, the card shows your initials.
  • Test contact card: on iPhone it opens the Create New Contact sheet. Android may show a download prompt first.
  • Click Save contact card.

👀 5. Preview what your leads see

The phone on the right shows your thank-you page and updates as you edit. Scroll inside the phone to see the whole page.

  • Sample lead state and Sample lead first name let you see how the page reads for different leads.
  • Your version includes unsaved changes. Leads see now shows what's live today.
  • Reset steps resets the two quick steps in the preview.
  • The preview needs a valid 10-digit US mobile number (your account number or your lead-text number) before it can show your page.
